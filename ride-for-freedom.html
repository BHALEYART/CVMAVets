Who We Ride For

MI Freedom Center

Since 2011 the Michigan Armed Forces Hospitality Center, also referred to as the MI Freedom Center, has welcomed our active duty military personnel, veterans, veterans' family members and friends while traveling together, dependents with identification, Guard, Reserve, NATO Force partners and Gold Star family members.

Ride for Freedom Donations

Can’t ride? You can still give.

Your donation supports the Ride for Freedom and advances its mission. Individuals, businesses, and community organizations are invited to contribute. Corporate contributors will receive special recognition on the day of the Ride.
