About Us

Having Our Six

The Combat Vets Auxiliary creates and fosters a caring and appreciative environment for CVMA® members.

CVMA® 35-3 Auxiliary

Mission statement

The mission of the CVMA®-Auxiliary Unit is to create and foster a caring and appreciative environment for CVMA® members. The focus of the CVMA®-Auxiliary is to help CVMA® accomplish its mission of Veterans Helping Veterans by offering support to CVMA® Veteran related causes on national and state levels.

The Auxiliary's main goal is to provide moral support and cultivate relationships; we want those who served and/or fought for this country to know they truly are respected and welcomed home.

What We Do

Objectives

  • Motorcycle activities associated with Veterans.
  • Creating and maintaining camaraderie among Veterans from all U.S. Branches of Services and its allies.
  • Supporting Veteran Organizations.
  • Raising awareness for the plight of POWs, MIAs and their families.
  • Encouraging a better understanding of motorcycle riders as a constructive sport among members of the press, and law enforcement agencies.
  • Promoting public awareness of Veterans Issues.
  • Conducting Auxiliary functions and activities in a manner befitting the members of the Combat Veterans Motorcycle Association, as well as assisting the CVMA with its functions and activities, either at the state or national level.

Who Can Join

Auxiliary membership requirements

Spouse of a Member

Must be the spouse of a CVMA® member or spouse of a deceased CVMA® member. A copy of your Marriage License or Marriage Certificate must accompany the Auxiliary application. Full members can request the Auxiliary Application from their State Representative.

Good Character

All members MUST conduct themselves in a manner that is becoming to themselves, to the CVMA and to the CVMA®-Auxiliary unit, and must not bring embarrassment upon themselves, the Auxiliary or the Association as a whole.

Support All Branches

Must support the efforts of ALL branches of the United States of America's Armed Forces and the Combat Veterans Motorcycle Association.
