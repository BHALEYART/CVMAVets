How to Join

Become a Member

Membership requires good character, a current motorcycle-endorsed driver’s license in your state of domicile, and owning and operating a motorcycle of 500cc or above.

Get the ApplicationEmail the Chapter

Membership Categories

Where do you fit?

Full Member

Combat veteran. Show your DD-214 (member 4 copy) as proof of creditable military service.

Requirements

Support Member

Non-combat veteran, applying through a sponsor who has held membership for at least one year.

Requirements

Auxiliary Member

Current spouse of a Full or Support Member. Submit your marriage certificate with the application.

About the Auxiliary

Proof of Service

Full & Support members

In order to apply to become a Full or Support member, you must show your DD-214 (member 4 copy) as proof of creditable military service.

Support Member Sponsorship

Veterans who did not deploy to a combat zone may apply as Support Members through a sponsor who has held membership for at least one year. Your sponsor must have:

  • Ridden 3,000 miles with you, or
  • Attended 3 CVMA events with you, or
  • Known you for at least 6 months.

The Process

From first ride to patching in

Come Meet Us

Attend a meeting or ride to make sure the chapter's values line up with yours. All vets are welcome to ride with us.

Apply

Submit your application, documentation and fee. Processing varies by National but typically takes no more than 30 days.

Patch In

Once you're approved, the chapter will coordinate your patching-in ceremony.

Questions

Membership FAQs

Still have questions? Email webmaster@cvmami35-3.org.

What proof do I need for Full Member eligibility?

You MUST have a DD-214 showing your combat service or verifiable, official documentation showing a combat award.

My DD-214 is missing or inaccurate. What do I do?

You can request your records from the National Archives: archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records.

Do I have to own a motorcycle?

Yes. You MUST own a motorcycle 500cc or larger and have a valid registration and insurance.

Do I have to be a member to attend events?

No. We welcome ALL vets to ride with CVMA 35-3 whether you are a member or not.

I want to be a Support Member but don't have a sponsor.

Come out to our meetings and events — that's the best way to meet members who can sponsor you.

How do I transfer my membership from another state?

Contact your current State Rep to start the transfer.
