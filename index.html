Our Mission

Support and defend those who defended our country

We are an Association of Combat Veterans from all United States Armed Forces branches who ride motorcycles as a hobby. CVMA® MI 35-3 is based in the area of Sterling Hgts., Macomb County Michigan, with members throughout southeast Michigan.

Our mission is to support and defend those who have defended our country and our freedoms — providing meals, clothing, shelter, and recognition to veterans and their families in need.

What We Wear

The meaning of our patch

Red

Represents the blood that has been shed on the battlefield.

Military Gold

Represents all branches of the military service of the United States.

Black

Represents the heavy hearts possessed for those who gave their lives and for those considered missing in action or prisoners of war.

♠

Skull & Ace of Spades

Represents the death that war leaves in its wake.
