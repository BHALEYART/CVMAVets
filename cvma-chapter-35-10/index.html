Who We Are

Support and protect those who defended our freedoms

CVMA® MI 35-10 is the Combat Veterans Motorcycle Association chapter based in Hemlock, Michigan — an Association of Combat Veterans from all branches of the United States Armed Forces who ride motorcycles as a hobby. Our mission is to support and protect those who have defended our country and our freedoms.

We provide assistance and help to individual veterans, veteran care facilities, other veteran organizations and registered charities, and we sponsor and take part in veteran-related motorcycle (and other) charity events throughout the year.

Our Membership

Three ways to belong

Full Members

Those with verified combat service.

Supporter Members

Those who have non-combat military service and have a strong dedication to helping veterans.

Auxiliary Members

Spouses of both Full and Support members.

Know the Difference

An association — not a motorcycle club

Please note that we are an ASSOCIATION and NOT a MOTORCYCLE CLUB (MC).

  • Although we may be in a geographic area, we do not claim territory.
  • We do not have a club house.
  • We do not wear cuts or colors, but rather vests.
  • The patch on our back is one piece.
