CVMA® Auxiliary Scholarship Program

Investing in our veterans’ families

The CVMA Auxiliary administers scholarships for members, their spouses, children, and grandchildren pursuing an Associate's degree or higher at an accredited institution. The program is funded by Auxiliary dues and generous donations.

The Combat Veterans Motorcycle Association (CVMA) Chapter 35-3 Auxiliary is a proud family of veterans, and our mission — “Vets Helping Vets” — guides our commitment to helping veterans and their families access higher education.

Why this cause matters: Education opens doors to opportunity, healing, and stability — but it often comes with financial obstacles. Helping families through school honors those who have sacrificed so much for our freedom.
