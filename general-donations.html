Where It Goes

How your donations are used

Helping Veterans in Need

Financial assistance for homelessness, medical expenses, or job transitions.

Charity & Fundraising

Organizing events and raising funds for veteran causes.

Healthcare Support

Medical care, mental health services, and treatment for service injuries.

Scholarships

Educational support for children and grandchildren of veterans.

Memorials & Ceremonies

Honoring veterans through events.

Veteran Advocacy

Legal assistance and benefits claims support.
